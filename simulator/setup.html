STEP 1 OF 4 AVAILABLE STEPS

Which system do you have?

Copy the details from your infotainment system's information screen. You can leave unknown details blank and plan now; they will be needed before a vehicle package can be qualified.

What if I don't know the generation?

Model year and screen size alone do not prove the generation or compatibility. Leave “Not sure yet” selected. Gen 2 is unsupported by the evidence reviewed here. Do not remove any hardware to complete this form.

Everything stays in this page until you explicitly export. No VIN, account, hardware removal or USB insertion is needed. Reloading clears the session.

STEP 2 OF 4 AVAILABLE STEPS

What would you like to change?

Choose ideas for your review plan. These are indexed AIO features, not a list of tweaks qualified for your car. No feature is selected by default.

    STEP 4 OF 4 AVAILABLE STEPS

    Save your review plan.

    The JSON file records selected feature descriptions, source references, generation and the firmware string you entered. It excludes model, year, region and market. It contains no installer or executable scripts.

    Save it on your computer. This preview cannot format or write a USB, and the download does not make a vehicle installation package.

MAZDA CONNECT GEN 1 ROOT — DESKTOP ALPHA

THIS DOWNLOAD IS NOT A VEHICLE INSTALLER. DO NOT PUT IT IN YOUR CAR.
It cannot prepare, format, write or discover a USB drive.

1. Extract this entire ZIP into a folder on your computer.
2. Open index.html in a current desktop browser. Automated checks use Chromium;
   Firefox and Safari have not been verified.
3. Follow Identify > Choose > Review > Save plan.
4. Save the JSON review plan on your computer if you want to keep it.

No Node.js, installation, server, account or internet connection is required
for the extracted desktop preview. External source links require internet
only when you follow them. Keep all extracted files together.

Unknown car details can stay blank. Do not guess the firmware or region.
No vehicle configuration is qualified in this alpha. USB preparation and
in-car installation are explicitly unavailable. There are no car scripts,
MP3 payloads, drive tools, firmware updates or vehicle instructions here.

The research tools link opens the AIO workbench and an in-memory mock
simulator. Mock terminal/reboot/region events do not reach a vehicle.

Data stays in page memory. Reloading clears the session. Explicit plan
export includes chosen feature metadata, generation and the entered firmware
string; it excludes the other target-identification fields. A download
request is not proof that your browser saved the file; check Downloads.

manifest.json records SHA-256 hashes of the extracted application files.
The separate SHA256SUMS.txt verifies the ZIP downloaded from GitHub.
